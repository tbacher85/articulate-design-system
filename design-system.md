# HealthCare E-Learning Design System - Thematic Color Framework

This document outlines how we adapt our Articulate Rise 360 modules by varying the accent and structural colors while maintaining a clean, consistent white/neutral general background across all medical training topics.

---

## 🏗️ Core Structural Rule (Global Base)
* **General Background:** Clean White / Warm Alabaster (`#F9F8F6` or `#FFFFFF`) — Ensures maximum legibility and consistency across all courses.
* **Global Text / Main Headers:** Deep Charcoal / Dark Neutral for optimal contrast.

---

## 🎨 Thematic Variations (Replacing the Classic Hospital Blue)

Instead of using a single blue for everything, we assign specific thematic palettes based on the medical topic to reinforce pedagogical context and emotional resonance:

### 1. Topic: Mental Health & Well-being
* *Objective:* Calm, reduce cognitive load, create a safe and empathetic space.
* **Primary Accent / Headers:** Soft Slate / Muted Teal (e.g., `#4A6B6C`)
* **Supporting Tone:** Warm Sand / Gentle Gray (`#F0EDE6`)

### 2. Topic: Clinical Care, Emergency & Procedures
* *Objective:* Precision, focus, acute attention to steps and protocols.
* **Primary Accent / Headers:** Deep Forest Sage / Slate Green (e.g., `#3B4D43`)
* **Supporting Tone:** Clean Light Gray (`#EFECE6`)

### 3. Topic: Prevention, Women's Health & Care Campaigns
* *Objective:* Warmth, human connection, approachability, empathy.
* **Primary Accent / Headers:** Terracotta / Muted Rose-Coral (e.g., `#D47A55` / `#C26747`)
* **Supporting Tone:** Soft Alabaster (`#F9F8F6`)

---

## 💡 Semantic Feedback Colors (Universal across all courses)
* **Success / Correct:** Olive Green (`#5B7053`)
* **Warning / Caution:** Warm Amber (`#C88A3B`)
* **Error / Critical:** Deep Brick (`#A94C38`)
