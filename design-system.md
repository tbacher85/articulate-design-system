# Healthcare E-Learning Color Reference Guide

This reference guide defines the psychological, clinical rationale, and exact HEX codes for our Articulate Rise 360 training modules. 

> **Core Design Principle:** While we maintain a clean white background (`#FFFFFF` / `#F9F8F6`) and neutral dark typography across all courses for consistency, **we swap the primary structural/accent color depending on the medical topic** to give each module the right clinical and emotional tone (moving away from a rigid, monolithic use of traditional blue).

---

## 🎨 Thematic & Structural Color Reference

🔵 **Blue — Calm, trust, hygiene, medical technology**
* **HEX Code:** `#3A6B88` (Muted Clinical Blue)
* **Why to use it:** Historically the benchmark color in healthcare, it establishes immediate institutional credibility and technical serenity without feeling intimidating.
* **When to use it:** For modules covering pharmacology, heavy medical devices, clinical anatomy, or rigorous institutional protocols. *(Can be replaced by other thematic accents depending on the topic).*

🟢 **Green — Health, healing, nature, soothing**
* **HEX Code:** `#3B4D43` (Deep Forest Sage)
* **Why to use it:** Evokes safety, regeneration, and life. It drastically reduces cognitive and visual fatigue—crucial for medical staff reviewing dense compliance or clinical procedures.
* **When to use it:** As a primary thematic accent for learning paths focused on patient prevention, routine nursing care, clinical nutrition, physical recovery, or therapeutic environments.

⚪ **White & Off-White — Cleanliness, sterility, simplicity, professionalism**
* **HEX Code:** `#FFFFFF` / `#F9F8F6` (Clean White & Warm Alabaster)
* **Why to use it:** Provides a neutral, distraction-free visual space, lowers cognitive load for busy healthcare professionals, and ensures optimal reading comfort.
* **When to use it:** As the universal primary background color across all Rise 360 blocks to guarantee complete visual consistency from one module to another.

🔘 **Light Gray — Structure, visual breathing room, neutrality**
* **HEX Code:** `#EFECE6` or `#F0F1F3` (Soft Structural Light Gray)
* **Why to use it:** Creates essential structural separation between sections and prevents visual fatigue, allowing busy medical staff to transition smoothly from theory to concrete examples.
* **When to use it:** For alternating block backgrounds in Rise 360, or inside structured components like *Tabs*, *Flashcards*, and quote boxes to organize complex clinical data cleanly.

🔴 **Red — Urgency, blood, danger, rapid intervention (Semantic / Feedback)**
* **HEX Code:** `#A94C38` (Deep Brick Red)
* **Why to use it:** Triggers an immediate physiological and cognitive reaction, signaling a critical priority that demands intense focus from medical personnel.
* **When to use it:** Strictly reserved for negative feedback in Knowledge Check quizzes, or for sections dealing with vital emergencies, acute risk management, and crisis protocols.

🟡 **Yellow / Amber — Attention, prevention, risk signaling (Semantic / Feedback)**
* **HEX Code:** `#C88A3B` (Warm Amber / Ochre)
* **Why to use it:** Captures attention effectively without the alarming, high-stress urgency of red, making it ideal for professional caution.
* **When to use it:** For warning blocks, moderate medication side effects, infection control precautions, or key clinical vigilance points that staff must look out for.

🟠 **Orange / Terracotta — Alert, energy, visibility, human warmth**
* **HEX Code:** `#D47A55` (Solar Terracotta)
* **Why to use it:** Combines high visibility with an approachable, human dimension that bridges the gap between clinical systems and empathetic patient care.
* **When to use it:** As a thematic accent or for interactive buttons (Call-to-Action) to guide healthcare staff through scenarios, especially in patient-provider communication modules.

🟣 **Purple — Specialized care, mental health, oncology, palliative care**
* **HEX Code:** `#6B4C7A` (Muted Amethyst / Deep Mauve)
* **Why to use it:** An introspective tone associated with complex, sensitive, or long-term medical specialties requiring high levels of empathy and emotional intelligence from staff.
* **When to use it:** As the primary thematic accent for modules dedicated to mental health, psychiatry, chronic pain management, psychological support, or end-of-life care.

🩷 **Pink — Targeted prevention, empathy, public health campaigns**
* **HEX Code:** `#C47685` (Soft Rose / Dusty Pink)
* **Why to use it:** Deeply anchored in compassionate care and public health awareness, it brings a gentle, non-threatening emotional warmth to sensitive medical topics.
* **When to use it:** As the primary thematic accent for specific staff awareness training on targeted screening campaigns, breast oncology, gentle pediatrics, or specialized women's health.

⚫ **Black / Dark Charcoal — Rigor, typography, formal structure**
* **HEX Code:** `#2B2B2B` (Dark Charcoal)
* **Why to use it:** Ensures absolute legibility and accessibility, allowing busy medical professionals to scan and absorb critical training content quickly.
* **When to use it:** Reserved strictly for main text bodies, structural headers, and essential structural elements across all modules.
